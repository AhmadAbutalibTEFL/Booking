Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BookMyClass (“we”, “us” or “the platform”), operated by Ahmad Abutalib, handles personal information when students, parents and independent teachers use BookMyClass. BookMyClass currently provides account, teacher-discovery, scheduling and booking features. It does not currently collect or process lesson payments.

In short: We use account and booking information to run the platform, show teachers the details they need to deliver booked lessons, protect accounts and prevent scheduling conflicts. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: information made available through Google sign-in, such as your name, email address and Google account identifier, plus information associated with your BookMyClass profile.
  • Teacher information: display name, biography, profile photo (if supplied), time zone, availability, lesson types and durations, minimum booking notice, and contact or meeting details configured by the teacher.
  • Booking information: student name, selected teacher and lesson type, booking date and time, booking status, and any optional phone number or notes submitted with a booking.
  • Technical information: information needed to authenticate your session and operate the website, which may include browser, device, and security-related data handled by our service providers.

2. How we use information

  • Create and secure accounts and authenticate users through Google.
  • Display teacher profiles, lesson options and available times.
  • Create, manage, change or cancel bookings and prevent double-booking.
  • Share relevant booking details with the teacher responsible for that booking.
  • Provide booking confirmations and facilitate lesson arrangements, including access details supplied by the teacher.
  • Maintain platform security, troubleshoot errors, enforce our Terms of Service and respond to support requests.

3. When information is shared

We share information only as needed to operate BookMyClass:

  • With the booked teacher: the teacher can access the student name and relevant booking details, including contact information or notes provided for that booking, so the teacher can arrange and deliver the lesson.
  • With service providers: Google provides sign-in/authentication services. Supabase provides database, authentication and hosting-related infrastructure for platform data. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation.
  • For legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users, or investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not currently process payments or payment-card details through BookMyClass.

4. Data storage and security

BookMyClass uses Supabase infrastructure to store and manage platform data and Google for account authentication. We use access controls intended to limit account and booking information to the relevant user, teacher and platform administrator. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a secure Google account and do not share your sign-in credentials.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account and booking information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the platform, maintain booking records, resolve disputes, protect security and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing us. We may need to retain limited information where the law requires it or where it remains necessary for legitimate security or dispute-resolution purposes.

6. Children and parent/guardian bookings

BookMyClass may be used to arrange lessons for children. A parent or legal guardian should create or manage the booking and provide the child's name where appropriate. Teachers and parents should avoid adding sensitive personal information to optional booking notes. We do not knowingly invite children to create independent accounts where parental involvement is required by applicable law.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on the use of your personal information. You can also stop using the platform and revoke Google account access through your Google Account settings. To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection rules in those countries may differ. Where required, we will take steps intended to provide an appropriate level of protection for transferred information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as BookMyClass changes. We will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to users' attention.
